## 💡 Why This Guide

> I've sat on both sides of the PM interview table — as a candidate at Google, Meta, and Apple, and as a hiring manager running 200+ interview loops at Amazon. What I saw was a gap: most interview prep is either outdated theory or unverifiable anecdotes.
>
> So I built this the way I'd build a product: start with data. I analyzed 2,445 real job descriptions, synthesized 100+ interview experiences, and extracted patterns from what actually gets people hired — not what sounds good in a blog post.
>
> This is not AI-generated filler dumped into a repo. Every insight comes from analyzing actual data and synthesizing patterns from it.

**What makes this different:**

- **Data-driven, not opinion-driven.** Every claim links to real job data, interview experiences, or practitioner sources
- **Covers the full lifecycle.** Not just "how to answer questions" — role analysis, skills gaps, career transitions, portfolio strategy, salary negotiation
- **Built for career switchers.** 5 specific transition paths (SWE, DS, Design, MBA, Consulting → PM) with timelines
- **Maintained and current.** 2025-2026 hiring patterns, AI PM trends, updated compensation data

Star this repo to keep an eye on updates.
