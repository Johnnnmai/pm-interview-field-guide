# Webinar 4: PM Take-Home Case Studies

- Date: March 9, 2026
- Host: [Alexey Grigorev](https://www.linkedin.com/in/agrigorev/)

## Description

Take-home case studies are where your ability to build production-ready product analyses is truly measured. This session focuses on moving beyond understanding requirements to mastering execution in case study assignments used by top-tier companies.

## Topics Covered

1. Case study analysis - examining recent Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 submissions to understand current PM hiring standards
2. Implementation discussion - deconstructing real-world assignment prompts and analyzing effective analytical approaches with trade-offs
3. End-to-end product analysis - building a complete solution for product-metrics tasks, emphasizing high-quality data analysis, metrics definition, and strategic recommendations

Practice resource: [The 100x Product Manager](../README.md) includes a full chapter on take-home case study execution with real examples, scoring rubrics, and common pitfalls that cost candidates offers.
